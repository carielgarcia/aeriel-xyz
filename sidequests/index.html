How to play

Three steps. None of them involve sitting down.

  1. Step 01 / 03

    Draw.

    Take the top card and read it. Out loud, if the music allows. The card tells you what the night needs from you.

  2. Step 02 / 03

    Find the person.

    A card might name a type of person, a small task or a dare for a friend. Locate them. They are in the room. They are always in the room.

  3. Step 03 / 03

    Hand it over.

    Complete the quest, earn the XP, then give the card to someone else. Cards are expendable. That is the point. Run out and buy another deck.

Every quest is small, silly and optional.

Rules of the room

  • Be kind. Nobody is the punchline. The quest is the punchline.

  • Ask, never push. A stranger can say no. A no ends the quest and counts as a good night.

  • Nothing unsafe. No drugs. No drinking requirements. Nothing that needs a yes you did not ask for.

  • Skip is always allowed. Draw again. The deck has 119 other opinions.

      Questions

      Questions, answered quickly

      Is this safe?

      Every quest is small, silly and optional. Nothing involves drugs, nothing requires a drink, and nothing needs a stranger to say yes before it can be completed. Skip is always allowed.

      Do I have to talk to strangers?

      Some cards say so. Skip them. Draw again. You can also hand any card to a friend, who will pretend they did not see it.

      Where do I play?

      Bars, clubs, raves, festivals, house parties, coat check lines, rooftops and the long walk to the metro. It also works at home, though the cards will be disappointed.

      What does “expendable” mean?

      You are meant to give cards away. Hand one to a stranger, a bartender or a friend and the quest goes with it. Run out and buy another deck. New editions add new quests to the pile.

      How do rarities work?

      Each deck has five tiers: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic and Legendary. The rarer the card, the bigger the quest and the bigger the XP. A tier is always printed as a word on the card, never as colour alone.

      Can I design a card?

      Maybe. Submissions open when this is a real store. Until then, keep your best dare to yourself and test it on someone you trust.
